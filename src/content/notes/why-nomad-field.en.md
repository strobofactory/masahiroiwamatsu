In an age when AI writes text and summarizes search results themselves, the meaning of writing a blog has changed.

If all you do is organize and publish general information, AI is faster. So here, instead of information anyone can generate, I will preserve **what I have actually seen, made, tried, and failed at**.

## Own the original source

Social media is convenient, but as a place to keep your own records, it is borrowed ground. When the service changes, how things are displayed and how they reach people change too.

A domain under my own name, on the other hand, can remain even as the themes change.

Video production, AI, apps, health, publishing, music, travel, coffee. Rather than splitting these up as though they were the work of different people, I want to preserve in one archive what one person thought over time and where that path led.

## NOMAD FIELD

NOMAD does not simply mean moving from place to place. It means not being fixed to one field or title, and moving toward the next place while verifying things for myself.

FIELD means the actual place where things happen.

I combined the two and named this site **NOMAD FIELD**.

The value of the information here will be determined not by the number of articles, but by its originality as a primary source.
